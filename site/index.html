One loop, four moves, every fifteen minutes.

01

Observe the live data

Every cycle the agent pulls the newest identification events from your subscription. Real visitors, real smart signals, right now. Not a sample from last quarter.

02

Verify the defense fired

Did the bot rule catch the bot burst? Did the velocity rule trip for the credential‑stuffing run, and only for that? The agent replays your rules against what just happened and grades them.

03

Tune the ruleset in place

When a rule under‑reaches or over‑reaches, the agent edits the ruleset on your Fingerprint workspace. Thresholds, conditions, actions, lists. Your application code is never touched.

04

Repeat, and check its own work

Then it watches the next window of traffic to confirm the fix landed. Every change is verified against live data, or it gets rolled back on the next pass.
